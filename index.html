05 / AI + HUMAN AGENCY

AI is a tool.
Human agency is the direction.

Neil's approach to AI in education is intentionally human-in-the-loop. AI can accelerate brainstorming, expand possibilities, and support refinement— but students remain responsible for judgment, reflection, decisions, and creation.

HUMAN NEED→AI ASSISTANCE→CRITICAL EVALUATION→HUMAN DECISION

09 / ABOUT

Building future-ready learners through meaningful work.

Neil Aldrin T. Olvina is a Licensed Professional Teacher and High School Technology and Livelihood Education educator at De La Salle Santiago Zobel School. He specializes in Electronics, Design & Technology, and Life Skills.

He is a textbook author, curriculum contributor, conference presenter, and educational technology practitioner. His professional work focuses on creating student-centered learning experiences that connect technical knowledge with creativity, collaboration, sustainability, and real-world problem solving.

His professional development includes Google, Microsoft, Minecraft Education, Apple, AI-focused learning, design learning, and international education networks.
